## What
Cinematic Capital's breakdown of Lionsgate's public, aggressive AI strategy — the most prominent studio openly embracing and promoting AI adoption.

## TL;DR
Lionsgate is the **most AI-forward major studio**: partnered with **Runway** (equity stake, custom model on its own library), ran **Artium AI** systems (ALICE box-office predictor, claimed **35% more accurate**), and became the **first studio to appoint a Chief AI Officer** (Kathleen Grace, reporting to CEO Jon Feltheimer) plus an **AI Steering Committee**. Claimed savings: **"tens and tens of millions of dollars a year."**

---

## 1) Partnerships with AI companies

### Runway
- **Sept 2024** — first-of-its-kind partnership: use Runway tools across **pre-visualization, storyboarding, final-frame production**
- **First studio** to partner with a gen-AI company to **train a custom AI model on its own library**
- **2026** — took an **equity stake** in Runway
- **Joint development program** to develop + produce new IP
- Slate of co-developed AI/content projects, starting with a **short-form episodic series** using Lionsgate IP + Runway gen models
- Lionsgate as presenting partner at the **Runway AI Festival** (filmmaker-focused events)

### Artium AI
- Built **ALICE** — predictive model with an intuitive UI for Lionsgate employees
- Claims **35% more accurate** at predicting box-office success **and** ideal casting choices
- Helped form a new **Data Strategy & Innovation team**
- Introduced a new cross-functional / cross-company collaboration approach
- Artium has also worked with **Disney** and **STARZ**

---

## 2) AI Department at Lionsgate

- **2026** — first studio to appoint a **Chief AI Officer: Kathleen Grace**
  - Oversees AI implementation in **pre-production and post-production**
  - Ex-**Vermillio** (AI platform for licensing/protecting IP + likeness)
  - Reports **directly to CEO Jon Feltheimer**
  - Sits on the studio's **senior decision-making team**
  - Mandate: build tools for filmmakers + protect studio and talent IP
- Created an **AI Steering Committee**

> AI will save the company **"tens and tens of millions of dollars a year"** across film and TV.
> — **Michael Burns, Lionsgate Vice Chairman, June 2026**

---

## Why It Matters
- Lionsgate = the **leading-edge public case** of a studio committing to gen AI (vs. peers staying quiet/litigious)
- Two-track approach: **Runway (content/creative)** + **Artium (analytics/prediction)** + **in-house CAIO (governance/IP)**
- CAIO reporting straight to the CEO signals AI as a **board-level/strategic** function, not an IT sub-unit
- IP/likeness protection is explicitly part of the CAIO mandate — talent-safe framing

## Relevance
- Benchmark for how studios structure AI orgs (CAIO + Steering Committee + external partnerships)
- Direct comparison point to Netflix/Eyeline GenFX approach (see `2026-10-11-netflix-eyeline-genfx-generative-ai-artist-judgment.md`)
- Useful for Sony: equity-stake + custom-model-on-library is the emerging studio play
