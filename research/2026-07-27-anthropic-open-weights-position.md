# Anthropic Position on Open-Weights Models
- **Source**: https://www.anthropic.com/news/position-open-weights-models
- **Author**: Dario Amodei, Anthropic CEO
- **Date**: Jul 27, 2026

## Key Findings

### Anthropic's Official Stance
- Anthropic has **never** advocated for a ban on open-weights models
- Open-weights models without dangerous capabilities = public good (free to run, value for businesses/developers/researchers)

### Two Nightmare Scenarios Amodei Worries About

1. **Authoritarian AI Superiority** — Authoritarian governments (esp. CCP) build AI more powerful than US models, use for permanent military superiority or deep domestic repression. Most dangerous model = one trained in secret and handed only to PLA/MSS. Irrelevant whether open or closed.

2. **AI-Enabled Attacks** — Powerful AI misused for cyberattacks, biological attacks, serious alignment problems. Open-weights models have higher risk because guardrails can't be enforced, usage can't be monitored, weights can't be withdrawn once released.

### Three Measures Anthropic Supports

1. **Chip export controls** — Don't sell powerful chips/chipmaking equipment to China. Crack down on rampant smuggling. China's limited domestic production + scaling laws = can't build more powerful models without US chips. Most efficient blocker for threat #1.

2. **Crack down on industrial-scale distillation** — Distillation is far more compute-efficient than training from scratch. Allows China to partially evade chip bans. Can bring Chinese frontier within months of US frontier. Key: open weights are less relevant than state-backed operations at the frontier. Anthropic has implemented account bans for distillation but it's a whack-a-mole (fake accounts, hard to detect).

3. **Mandatory safety testing for all capable models** — Open AND closed, regardless of country of origin. Test for cyber, biological, and alignment risks before release. Should be global (CCP must be on board). Trump administration moving in this direction. Industry proposals exempt less capable models (startups/academia).

### On the Open Letter (NVIDIA/tech companies)
- Amodei agrees: open weights expand access, strengthen competition, give customers control
- Distillation concerns should be addressed via targeted legal/commercial frameworks (not blanket bans)
- **Disagrees** with assertions that:
  - Open weights necessarily make safeguards easier to develop
  - Broad access necessarily helps defenders more than attackers

### Biology: Attacker-Defender Asymmetry
- Biology may have strong attacker advantage
- Sufficiently capable models could quickly weaponize pandemic-level viruses with widely available materials
- Defense (like Operation Warp Speed) takes years
- Currently safe because negative correlation between capability and desire to cause catastrophic harm — AI may break this correlation
- Powerful technology removes all barriers, exposes structural advantage

### Distillation Mechanics
- Compute-efficient process vs. training from scratch
- Backed by authoritarian state (not just commercial actors)
- Challenging to enforce: accounts only identified after substantial distillation; creates large numbers of fake accounts
- Individual company practices insufficient — need policy
