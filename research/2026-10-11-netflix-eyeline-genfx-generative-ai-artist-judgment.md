## What
James Rustad (Global Head of GenFX at **Eyeline**, Netflix's in-house VFX studio) presented on generative AI workflows in filmmaking at an **InnoAsia** session — *"Making What's Next: Generative AI and the Future of Filmmaking at Eyeline"* — at the **Busan International Film Festival** (Asian Contents & Film Market).

## TL;DR
**Generative AI widens the options for a VFX shot; artists decide which options serve the story.** Rustad deliberately steered away from "what AI can't replace" (technology moves too fast) toward *"where do generative tools belong and which tasks should they handle today."*

---

## Key Facts

- **Case study:** Netflix's *"Brazil '70: The Third Star"*
  - **1,300+ VFX shots**, incl. **800+ set on the soccer field**
- **Team:** Eyeline works with **45 artists** on a **hybrid approach** — generative tools + established VFX techniques
- **Venue:** Busan Intl. Film Festival / Asian Contents & Film Market

---

## The 4-Stage Generative AI Shot Workflow

| Stage | What happens |
|-------|-------------|
| **1. Setup** | Prompts, reference gathering, initial tests → establishes the options available |
| **2. Narrative** | Results assessed **against the story** — the artist-judgment gate |
| **3. Look** | Visual refinement: lighting, scene details, period accuracy |
| **4. Delivery** | Compositing, upscaling, bit-depth checks, technical review |

### The core insight
> The workflow places a **narrative decision between initial experimentation and visual finishing** — moving from *"what's possible"* to *"what works for the story."*

That step — selecting which generated results become finished shots — is where **artists' judgment** is irreducible.

Rustad: **"Artists' judgments are also important."**

## Why It Matters
- Reframes the AI-vs-artists debate from *replacement* to **option generation + editorial judgment**
- Netflix is building AI into VFX through **Eyeline** (in-house studio), not outsourcing — signals internal capability build
- The "hybrid" framing (45 artists + gen tools) is a concrete staffing model, not a hypothetical
- Emphasizes **narrative judgment as the bottleneck**, not generation capacity

## Relevance
- Model for how studios are structuring gen-AI VFX pipelines (setup → narrative gate → look → delivery)
- The "narrative gate" between experimentation and finishing is the reusable design pattern
- Useful precedent for AI-in-production workflows at Sony
