# Prague Studios — Strategic Analysis: AI Grey-Box

> Is it a good idea for Prague Studios to invest in AI Grey-Box technology?
> Considering they already attract productions like Foundation

---

## The Honest Answer

**Yes — but not to replace Foundation-tier productions. To capture the productions that can't afford Foundation.**

## What AI Grey-Box Adds

### Not a Replacement — An Addition

| Existing Business | AI Grey-Box Addition |
|-------------------|---------------------|
| Foundation-tier ($45-75M) | Mid-tier ($10-30M) |
| 120+ day shoots | 21-30 day shoots |

### The Market Gap

**Foundation-tier productions:** $50-100M, 120+ days → Prague gets these, but rarely

**Mid-tier productions:** $10-50M, 30-60 days → **THIS is where the volume is**
- Independent sci-fi films
- Streaming series (not flagship)
- International co-productions
- Music videos (complex)
- Commercial campaigns
- Corporate content

**Low-budget productions:** $1-10M, 10-30 days → **AI Grey-Box ideal**
- Emerging filmmakers
- Film school projects
- Test shoots
- Proof of concept

| Market rejects AI | Low | Medium | Position as tool, not replacement |

| Competition emerges | High | Low | First-mover advantage, Academy moat |

### What If Foundation Leaves?

| Scenario | Impact | AI Grey-Box Mitigation |
|----------|--------|----------------------|
| Apple cancels | -40% revenue | Mid-tier productions fill gap |
| Budget cuts | -20% revenue | Fixed costs already covered |
| Move to another city | -100% Foundation revenue | Diversified client base |

### The Real Risk

**Not investing in AI Grey-Box:**
- Continued dependence on 1-2 flagship productions
- Vulnerable to client decisions
- Competing on price, not capability
- Missing the mid-tier market entirely

## Recommendation

### Phase 1: Prove the Concept (Months 1-6)
- Build 1 AI Grey-Box stage (not full facility)
- Run 2-3 test productions (low-budget)
- Validate workflow, quality, turnaround
- Measure actual costs vs projections

### Phase 2: Scale if Validated (Months 7-12)
- Expand to full AI Grey-Box capability
- Launch Academy
- Target 3-5 mid-budget productions
- Establish partnerships (UPS, Sony, PFX)

### Phase 3: Integrate with Foundation (Year 2+)
- Offer AI pre-production to Foundation-tier productions
- "Foundation uses our AI for storyboards and previz"
- Best of both worlds: prestige + efficiency
