# Prague Studios — Hybrid Virtual Production Proposal

> Combined from: Hive VP Studio concept, Doug Liman's AI Gray Box methodology, Sony Academy LATAM strategy, and Sony Annex A operational model.
> Prepared for: Prague Studios Hybrid VP Facility

---

## Executive Summary

Prague Studios proposes a **two-stage hybrid virtual production facility** that combines:

1. **Stage A — LED Volume (ICVFX)**: Traditional real-time in-camera VFX with ~100 reconfigurable LED panels (Sony or ROE), serving as the proven, known-quantity offering
2. **Stage B — AI Grey-Box Stage**: A minimal-build stage where actors perform against grey/green, with environments generated and extended using AI — based on the methodology proven by Doug Liman's $70M "Bitcoin" production

The differentiator is not two rooms for rent — it's a **unified pipeline** where productions can block and previz on the grey-box stage, do hero LED work on Stage A, and share a generative/splatting toolchain across both.

### Strategic Partnership: Sony Virtual Production Ecosystem

Prague Studios aligns with Sony's Virtual Production Academy strategy, leveraging:
- **Ocellus** camera tracking system
- **VENICE** cinema cameras
- **Unreal Engine** plugins and integration

## The Doug Liman Precedent: Why This Works

Doug Liman's "Bitcoin" ($70M budget) validated the AI grey-box approach at scale:

| Metric | Traditional | AI Gray Box (Bitcoin) |
|--------|-------------|----------------------|
| Below-the-line cost | $120-130M | $40M ($25M starting today) |
| Pre-production | 7-8 months, $15-20M | 30 days or less |
| Physical shoot | 72 days | 21 days |
| Locations | 213 across 18 countries | 1 gray stage |
| Setup turnaround | Hours | 5-20 minutes |
| Day players | Standard | 130+ (more than any Liman film) |

### Key Technical Insights from Bitcoin

- **Gray stage with specific lighting** — consistent, designed for AI capture, not for human eye
- **AR previsualization** — director sees photoreal environments projected over the gray stage in real-time
- **Proprietary system with 60 patents** — handles storyboards, first frames, location builds, scheduling, shot breakdowns
- **Performance capture** — actors are real, director directs normally, camera work is standard
- **AI builds the world around captured actors** — not replacing actors, replacing sets
- **Department heads more important than ever** — they craft the vision in pre and post, less involved during shoot
- **Savings is in time, not people** — full freight paid to actors, director, department heads

### What Liman Learned About AI Companies

> "We met with every AI company — Runway, Midjourney, you name it. We left every meeting with the same outcome: they are never gonna get it, at least not in the short term. AI is 50% tech and 50% art. Studios hiring AI chiefs without hands-on production experience — that was never gonna work."

**Prague Studios advantage**: We combine production expertise with AI capability, not the other way around.

## Stage B — AI Grey-Box Stage

### The Bitcoin Model, Adapted

Based on Doug Liman's proven methodology:

1. **Pre-production (30 days or less)**
   - Input script → AI generates storyboards, first frames, location builds
   - Every shot pre-planned with scheduling, sides, VFX breakdown
   - Department heads craft the vision before a single frame is shot
   - Director watches the "whole movie" as AI-generated preview

2. **Production (70% time reduction)**
   - Gray stage with consistent, AI-optimized lighting
   - AR system projects photoreal environments over the stage
   - Director directs normally — standard cameras, standard crew
   - Actors perform with real props (AI tells you which props to use physically)
   - 5-20 minute turnarounds between setups
   - All 130+ day players shot in under 2 days

3. **Post-production**
   - AI builds the world around captured actors
   - Department heads coordinate through the system
   - Any element can be modified: lighting, locations, environments
   - 10 minutes shown at Cannes 2 weeks after wrapping (before having a cut)

### Technical Pipeline

```
Script Input
    ↓
AI Pre-production
    ├── Storyboards
    ├── First frames (every shot)
    ├── Location builds (architectural perfection)
    ├── Shot scheduling
    ├── VFX breakdown per shot
    └── Full movie preview (watchable, not release quality)
    ↓
Gray Stage Shoot
    ├── AR previsualization (director sees final environment)
    ├── Performance capture (real actors, real cameras)
    ├── AI tells crew which props to use physically
    └── 5-20 min turnarounds
    ↓
AI Post-production
    ├── World generation around actors
    ├── Environment extension via 3D Gaussian Splatting
    ├── Lighting/color matching
    ├── Fast draft render (editorial starts immediately)
    └── Overnight final-pixel render (4K, 16-bit)
```

### Gaussian Splatting Integration

3D Gaussian Splatting serves as a **visualization layer**, not a render step:

- **Preproduction**: Previz environments before the shoot
- **On-Set (Simulcam)**: Live preview so director/DP see composited shot in real time
- **Pipeline feed**: Same splatting data feeds both Stage A (LED) and Stage B (grey-box)

## The Pipeline: Stronger Together

The strategic advantage is using both stages as a unified system:

### Workflow
1. **Grey-box first** — Block scenes, test generative environments, iterate without burning LED wall time
2. **LED for hero work** — Final environments, lighting, and camera moves land on Stage A for in-camera final pixel
3. **Shared toolchain** — Creative and technical decisions from grey-box carry forward into LED work — no starting over

### Delivery Tiers
| Tier | Speed | Quality | Use Case |
|------|-------|---------|----------|
| Fast Draft | Hours | Preview quality | Editorial starts cutting immediately |
| Final Pixel | Overnight | 4K, 16-bit, layered passes or final pixel | Delivery to client |

### The Mid-Budget Sweet Spot

From Liman's experience:
> "The movie we made — original IP, unproven — they just don't get made anymore, especially in the mid-budget range. There's a lot of talented people with stories to tell, and that's what Hollywood was built on. Not sequel, prequel, tentpole, rinse, repeat."

Prague Studios can capture this underserved market by offering:
- **More creative control** for directors
- **Less time involved** in production
- **100% photoreal results** at a fraction of the cost
- **Proof points before greenlight** — show studios what the movie looks like before spending $100M
