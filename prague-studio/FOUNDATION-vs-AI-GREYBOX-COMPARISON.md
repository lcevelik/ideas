6. **Talent costs** — A-list actors, producers, directors
7. **Time** — 120+ shooting days + months of prep + months of post

## Where AI Grey-Box Wins

### 1. Set Construction (Saved: $4-7M)
**Foundation:** Builds physical sets for each planet, location, environment
**AI Grey-Box:** AI generates environments around actors on gray stage

**Example:**
Foundation builds a Trantor palace = $2-3M construction
AI Grey-Box generates Trantor palace = $0.1M (compute only)

### 2. Location Costs (Saved: $2.5-4.5M)
**Foundation:** Films in Prague Studios + 20-50 Czech locations
**AI Grey-Box:** Films on 1 gray stage, AI adds locations

**Example:**
Foundation films at Troja Palace, Libochovice Castle, Brno water tanks = permits, logistics, crew transport
AI Grey-Box generates same locations digitally = $0 additional cost

### 3. Shooting Time (Saved: $7-12M)
**Foundation:** 120+ days of shooting
**AI Grey-Box:** 21-30 days of shooting

**Example:**
Foundation: 120 days × 300 crew × $200/day = $7.2M crew cost
AI Grey-Box: 25 days × 100 crew × $200/day = $0.5M crew cost

### 4. Setup Turnarounds (Saved: $2-4M in time)
**Foundation:** Hours between setups (move equipment, relight, reset)
**AI Grey-Box:** 5-20 minutes (no physical changes needed)

**Example:**
Foundation: Moving from "Trantor Palace" to "Terminus Desert" = 4 hours × 300 crew = lost productivity
AI Grey-Box: AI changes environment instantly = 0 lost time

## Where Foundation Wins (AI Grey-Box Limitations)

### 1. Physical Tangibility
**Foundation:** Actors touch real walls, sit on real furniture, interact with real props
**AI Grey-Box:** Actors interact with gray blocks, AI adds details later

**Impact:** Some performances benefit from physical environment

### 2. Established IP & Talent
**Foundation:** Asimov's Foundation, A-list cast, Apple backing
**AI Grey-Box:** Needs to prove itself with original content first

**Impact:** Foundation attracts top talent and guaranteed audience

### 3. VFX Quality
**Foundation:** $8-15M VFX budget, established VFX houses
**AI Grey-Box:** AI-generated environments may have artifacts

**Impact:** Foundation currently looks better (but gap is closing)

### 4. Risk Profile
**Foundation:** Proven methodology, known costs
**AI Grey-Box:** New methodology, uncertain quality

**Impact:** Studios prefer known quantities for greenlighting
