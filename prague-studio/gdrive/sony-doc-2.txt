1. Purpose of This Annex 
This document complements the strategic proposal previously submitted by 
providing concrete evidence of the operational, financial, and technical 
experience of the Virtual Production Academy and HCG Technologies team 
in building, evangelizing, and scaling technology ecosystems across Latin 
America. 
The objective of this annex is to demonstrate: 
• 
Proven capability for regional execution 
• 
Financial models that are already in operation 
• 
Measurable results across education, marketing, and sales 
• 
Direct alignment with the Virtual Production ecosystem and camera-
tracking technologies 
 
2. Validated Operational Model (Vicon LATAM Case) 
2.1 Current Financial Model 
We currently operate as Vicon’s regional strategic partner under the following 
structure: 
Monthly Retainer 
• 
USD 10,200 
Annual Marketing Budget 
• 
USD 20,000 
• 
Dedicated to Latin America 
• 
Used for the organization and sponsorship of industry events (USD 
1,500–2,000 per event), including summits, workshops, and industry 
activations 
Annual Travel Budget 
• 
USD 18,000 
• 
Allocated to flights and accommodation for strategic events across 
Latin America 
All expenses are fully documented and justified through monthly Excel 
reports, which are reviewed in periodic meetings with Vicon’s regional 
channel management. 
 
2.2 Commission Model 
• 
30% commission for projects in Mexico and OEM projects integrated 
by our team worldwide 
• 
30% commission for Virtual Production and Entertainment projects in 
Latin America 
In these cases, additional margins are negotiated with local distributors, who 
typically come from engineering, medical sciences, or advanced systems 
integration sectors.
