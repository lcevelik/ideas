# Prague Studios — Restructured Sony Partnership Proposal

> Built on the LATAM playbook. Not a proposal — a business plan.

---

## Executive Summary

Prague Studios proposes to become **Sony's European Virtual Production Academy and Production Hub** — combining a physical VP facility with an education-driven market development strategy that follows the exact model proven in LATAM.

The difference: LATAM built a network of distributors. Prague builds a **physical destination** that generates revenue from day one while training the European VP workforce.
