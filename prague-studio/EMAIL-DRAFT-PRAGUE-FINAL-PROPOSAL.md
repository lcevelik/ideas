This is the final proposal for Prague Studios' AI Grey-Box Stage within the HIVE facility — a production-ready implementation plan leveraging our resident partners and proven methodology.

THE CONCEPT

Prague Studios will operate a two-stage hybrid virtual production facility within HIVE:

1. Stage A — LED Volume (ICVFX)
   Sony Verona/Capri LED panels, Ocellus tracking, VENICE cameras. The proven, known-quantity offering for hero shots and final pixel.

2. Stage B — AI Grey-Box Stage
   Minimal practical build, actors on grey/green, environments generated and extended by AI. Based on validated methodology from major studio productions.

The differentiator: both stages share a unified pipeline. Productions block and previz on Stage B, do hero LED work on Stage A, and creative decisions carry forward without starting over.

THE HIVE ADVANTAGE

Prague Studios has something most facilities don't: equipment and VFX partners already in the building.

| Partner | Role | What They Provide |
|---------|------|-------------------|
| Universal Partners Services | Lighting & Grip | Full equipment package, crew, on-set support |
| Sony (resident) | Technology | Ocellus, VENICE, LED panels, UE plugins |
| PFX | VFX | Final pixel, compositing, color, delivery |

This means:
- No capital expenditure on lighting/grip (UPS provides)
- No need to build VFX capability (PFX handles it)
- Sony equipment available from day one
- Academy workshops use real production gear
- Complete ecosystem without building from scratch

WHAT WE'RE BUILDING

Stage B — AI Grey-Box Stage

A gray stage with consistent, AI-optimized lighting where actors perform against minimal practical sets. The AI generates and extends environments around the captured performance.

Technical specifications:
- Gray stage with consistent lighting (doesn't change)
- Blocking shapes on walls (AI-optimized capture)
- AR previsualization system (director sees final environment)
- Multiple cameras (standard operation)
- Computing infrastructure for AI generation

The workflow:
- Pre-production: 30 days or less (script → storyboards → first frames → location builds → full movie preview)
- Production: 70% time reduction (5-20 minute turnarounds, no set building)
- Post-production: Fast draft render (editorial starts immediately) → PFX handles final pixel
