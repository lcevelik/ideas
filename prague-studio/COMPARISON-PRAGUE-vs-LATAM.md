## The 7 Reasons Sony Picks LATAM

### 1. It's Already Working

LATAM proposal says: "Here's what we've done with Vicon, Epic, Rokoko. Here are the numbers. Here are the distributors. Here are the universities."

Prague proposal says: "Here's what we COULD do if you give us the hardware."

Sony doesn't fund experiments. They fund proven models.

### 2. Scale > Single Point

LATAM: 6 countries, 12 distributors, 50+ universities, 10,000+ people
Prague: 1 studio, 1 city, 0 existing relationships

From Sony's perspective: why invest in one location when you can own a region?

### 3. Education IS the Strategy (Not a Side Project)

LATAM proposal structure:
- Level 1: Awareness → Level 2: Enablement → Level 3: Certification → Level 4: Sales
- Every level feeds the next
- The Academy IS the business

Prague proposal structure:
- Academy is 1 of 3 pillars (Academy + Production + R&D)
- Academy supports the studio, not the other way around
- No clear path from education to revenue

Sony needs market DEVELOPMENT. LATAM delivers that. Prague delivers a studio that happens to have a classroom.

### 4. The Team Has Credibility

LATAM team:
- Carlos Vilchis — ILM Sr. Mocap TD, Unreal Authorized Instructor, 12 years VP
- Hans Guevara — Virtual humans pioneer, Mass Challenge finalist, NVIDIA Inception
- Ricardo Ortiz — Chaos product specialist, 150+ technical texts, 70K+ followers

Prague team:
- The Globe (VP expertise) — no public track record mentioned
- Milkshake (agency) — €100/hr, "scope pending further discussion"

Sony needs people who can stand on stage at IBC and say "we built this." LATAM has that. Prague has "we will build this."

### 5. Revenue Model Is Clear

LATAM:
- $10,200/month retainer (fixed cost for Sony)
- $20K/year marketing budget
- $18K/year travel budget
- 30% commission on projects
- All expenses documented in monthly Excel reports

Prague:

- No retainer structure
- No commission model
- No budget breakdown
- "Cost: €100 blended hourly rate, scope pending"

Sony finance teams love predictable costs. LATAM gives them a line item. Prague gives them a question mark.

### 6. Distribution Already Exists

LATAM:

Prague:

### 7. Community = Proof of Demand

LATAM:

## What Prague Does Better

To be fair:

1. **Physical facility** — Prague Studios is a real place with real stages. LATAM is a distributed network.
2. **R&D Lab** — Sony gets a testing environment for new gear. LATAM doesn't offer this.
3. **European location** — Central Europe is closer to Sony's European HQ than LATAM.
4. **Production pipeline** — Prague offers actual VP production services, not just education.

---

## The Fundamental Gap

**LATAM proposal answers:** "How do we sell Sony VP technology in a market that doesn't know it exists?"

**Prague proposal answers:** "How do we build a VP studio that uses Sony gear?"

These are different questions. Sony needs the first one answered. The second one is nice but doesn't solve their core problem: market creation.

---

## What Prague Would Need to Compete

1. **Prove the model works** — Run 5-10 productions, publish results
2. **Build community first** — 1,000+ members before asking Sony for hardware
3. **Get distributor commitments** — Letters of intent from European resellers
4. **Define the financial model** — Retainer, commission, budget — not "scope pending"
5. **Show educational track record** — Certifications issued, students trained, instructors authorized
6. **Team credentials** — ILM, Epic, or major studio backgrounds on the team

Until then, LATAM wins because it's a business, and Prague is a proposal.
